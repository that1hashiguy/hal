2 · Use identities

From certificates to a protected connection

The workloads present their Vault-issued identities, verify each other, and establish session keys through hybrid key exchange.

Short-lived workload identities

Workload certificates expire after 10 seconds by default and rotate automatically at about half their lifetime. Test the connection to observe the frontend certificate’s remaining validity. The backend certificate’s expiry is not exposed by the proxy.
